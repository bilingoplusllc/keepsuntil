    What the label prints

    Every window here comes from the USDA FoodKeeper data, unchanged. Three things are worked out on top of it: how much longer the freezer buys you, what opening the packet costs, and the calendar date your particular jar runs out.

    These are quality windows, not safety guarantees. Food can spoil before the date if it was warm on the way home, and a swollen can or an off smell beats any number on this page. When in doubt, throw it out.
