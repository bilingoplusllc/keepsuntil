Two counts, two starting days

The five years and the three to four days are not one clock running down. The pantry count starts the day the can comes home; the fridge count starts the moment you open it, no matter how long the can sat sealed first. A can opened in its fifth year still gets its three to four days.

USDA FoodKeeper lists no freezer window for canned chicken, so this label does not print one. Some items here gain months in the freezer; this one has no such entry to quote.

Read this before you trust the date

These are quality windows from the USDA FoodKeeper data, not safety guarantees, and they assume a fridge at 40 °F or below and a pantry that stays cool and dry. A can that is bulging, leaking, badly dented at the seam or rusted through goes in the bin whatever the date says, and so does anything that smells wrong when opened. When in doubt, throw it out.
