Opening the can cuts what is left by 456 times: 1,825 days become 4. Nothing else on this page matters as much as which of the two dates you filled in.

What the two dates mean. Bought on: the day the sealed can came home; FoodKeeper counts its pantry life from purchase, not from the printed code. Opened on: the day the can was opened and the contents moved to a covered container in the fridge. If the can is still sealed, leave the second box empty.

These are quality dates, not safety guarantees. They assume the pantry is cool and dry and the fridge is at 40°F or below. A bulging, leaking or badly dented can, or anything that smells wrong when opened, goes in the bin whatever the date says.
