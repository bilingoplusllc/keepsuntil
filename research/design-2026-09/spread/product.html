Canned chicken

In the cupboard, unopened, this is one of the longest-lasting things you own: the USDA gives it five years from the day you bought it.

The moment the lid comes off, the same food is on a three-to-four-day clock in the fridge. Nothing about the chicken changed. The container did.

Why the gap

What the lid
was doing

A sealed can is not merely a closed container. It was heated after it was filled, and it holds no living organisms and no fresh air. There is nothing inside to spoil the food and nothing getting in. What limits the five years is slow chemical change — texture, colour, flavour drifting — rather than anything that would make you ill.

Opening it undoes every one of those conditions at once. Air, moving hands, a spoon, the air of the kitchen: the food goes from sterile and sealed to an ordinary cooked poultry dish, and cooked poultry in a fridge is a three-to-four-day proposition. That is exactly what the FoodKeeper figure says. It is not a stricter rule for canned chicken; it is the normal rule for the food that was inside.

This is why the two figures cannot be added, averaged, or read as a range. They describe two different foods in two different states, and only one of them is on your worktop.

Read this before you rely on a date

Where these
figures stop

  • These are quality figures, not safety guarantees. The five years describe how long the contents stay good, not a day on which they turn. Nothing here overrides how the food looks and smells.
  • The can decides before the calendar does. A can that is bulging, leaking, badly dented at a seam, or rusted through is thrown out whatever date this page prints.
  • The five years count from purchase, not from packing. That is the FoodKeeper's own basis for this entry, and it is why the field above asks when you bought it.
  • The three-to-four days assume a cold fridge. 40°F / 4°C or below, contents covered. A warmer fridge is a shorter figure, and this page cannot tell you how much shorter.
  • The freezer is not covered for this food. The FoodKeeper carries no freezer figure for canned chicken, so this page prints none. An absent figure is not permission.

Source. USDA FoodKeeper, entry “Canned chicken”, category Poultry. Both spans are reproduced in the source's own wording: pantry from purchase, 5 years; refrigerator after opening, 3 days to 4 days. FoodKeeper data is published by the U.S. Department of Agriculture and is in the public domain.
